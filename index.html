The Thesis
Most manifestation apps are glorified affirmation widgets. Assumed is the only tool that treats the body as the primary site of belief change.
The Law of Assumption (Neville Goddard) posits that sustained identity-level assumptions, not affirmations, drive reality shifts. Assumed operationalizes this through a breathwork-anchored assumption practice: users don't just write their desires, they physiologically anchor them. This mechanism has no direct competitor in the current app market.
